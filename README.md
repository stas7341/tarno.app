> Keep your WhatsApp groups safe, spam-free, and organized 24/7 without manual admin burnout.

🌐 **Website:** [https://tarno.app](https://tarno.app)

---

## 💡 What is Tarno?

**Tarno** is a command center built specifically for WhatsApp group admins, community leaders, and multi-group networks. 

Managing large WhatsApp communities natively is difficult: spammers invade during off-hours, join queues pile up, and tracking repeat offenders requires constant manual effort. Tarno pairs via standard WhatsApp Linked Devices in seconds—requiring no Meta Cloud API setup—and handles group safety automatically.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

* **🛡️ 24/7 Spam & Link Shield:** Auto-detects and removes unapproved links, crypto scams, and blacklist keywords instantly.
* **⚖️ 3-Strike Automation:** Configurable automated warning engine that issues strikes and kicks chronic offenders.
* **📁 Multi-Group Clusters:** Aggregate separate sister groups (Announcements, General, Regional) into a single unified management hub.
* **🔍 Cross-Group Roster Search:** Search contacts and participants across every group simultaneously and export rosters to CSV.
* **📋 Smart Join Approvals & Waitlists:** Organize incoming join requests with priority tagging (VIPs, residents, members).

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit [tarno.app](https://tarno.app) and create your account.
2. Link your WhatsApp session via QR code (Linked Devices) in 10 seconds.
3. Select your groups, configure moderation rules, and let Tarno manage the heavy lifting.
